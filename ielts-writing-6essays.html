讨论双方（Discuss both views）— 远程办公

题型：Discuss both views 核心框架：dilemma → I would argue
Some people think that it is better to work from home, while others believe that working in an office is more beneficial. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

As technology advances, more and more people have the chance to work remotely. Whether it is better to work from home or office has always been a dilemma for individuals. I would argue that it is more advantageous to work from home.

The option to work in an office is attractive for several reasons. Most office employees are required to show up for work at a fixed time, which means they have to commute by public transport or drive themselves. As a result, they must maintain a regular sleep schedule, which can actually promote a healthier daily routine. Another reason is that people tend to be more productive in an office environment. This is because colleagues and supervisors are physically present, which creates a sense of accountability and reduces procrastination.

On the other hand, I believe that it is more beneficial to work from home. Firstly, remote work does not require physical presence. For example, since individuals do not need to commute, they can save time and energy, therefore having more time to be with their family. Secondly, working from home offers greater flexibility, which improves job satisfaction. For instance, employees can design their own schedule to work at their best state, thus enhancing their productivity. This way of adjustment always leads to higher motivation and lower stress levels.

In conclusion, for the reasons mentioned above, it seems to me that people working at home are more likely to experience job satisfaction. This is mainly because remote work not only saves commuting time and allows for more family time, but also reduces social stress commonly found in an office environment.

同意/不同意（Agree/Disagree）— 免费大学

题型：Agree/Disagree 核心框架：While I acknowledge… I believe…
Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, regardless of their financial background. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Some people hold a view that college tuition fees should be free for all students, regardless of their financial background. While I acknowledge the benefits of this policy, I believe that the disadvantages would outweigh the advantages in the long run.

On the one hand, I accept that it is crucial to make university free for all individuals. In most communities, there are people who are impoverished and disadvantaged in some way. It is possible for those with low-income backgrounds to go to university if they do not need to pay for tuition fees. In this way, it would heavily reduce the financial burden on their families. This would undoubtedly give them a better starting point in life.

At the same time, I believe that the drawbacks of free university education are much more significant. In some countries, the cost of running universities is already extremely high, and making them free would impose an unbearable burden on national expenditure as well as taxpayers. For example, when governments spend more money on education, they may be forced to reduce funding for healthcare and infrastructure, as the overall budget is limited. Although a small reduction in tuition fees might seem helpful in the short term, in the long run, it would have a much greater negative impact on other essential public services. Moreover, making education entirely free does not necessarily guarantee that the most disadvantaged students will benefit the most, as wealthy families often have better access to preparatory resources and elite institutions.

In conclusion, it is true that free university education sounds beneficial for students from disadvantaged backgrounds, but in my opinion, the financial strain it places on governments and taxpayers makes it an impractical policy. After all, public funds are limited, and they should be allocated in a way that benefits the majority of society.

同意/不同意（Agree/Disagree）— 铁路 vs 公路

题型：Agree/Disagree 核心框架：While… are undeniably important, I disagree…
Some people believe that governments should spend more money on railways rather than on roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

While railways are undeniably important for transport between cities, I disagree with the view that governments should prioritise funding on railways over road infrastructure.

On the one hand, I acknowledge that railways could benefit individuals in specific scenarios. High-speed trains can transport large numbers of passengers over long distances efficiently. For example, in China, some companies have business operations in both Beijing and Shanghai, which means their employees often need to commute between these two cities efficiently and sometimes work during the journey. As railways offer comfortable carriages with tables and power outlets, business travellers can work with great ease. Since railways are more efficient for business travel, I can understand why some people feel that we should prioritise railway infrastructure.

At the same time, road construction is significantly more inclusive and cost-effective than railway expansion, especially in undeveloped areas. The cost of building a kilometre of high-speed rail far outweighs the cost of road building. For example, building two-lane roads is far more affordable and can immediately connect remote villages to towns, making it easier for children to attend school, farmers to transport their crops to markets, and patients to reach hospitals. Without such road investment, these communities would remain isolated, widening the gap between urban and rural areas. In this way, roads deliver massive benefits to ordinary citizens in their daily lives, especially in rural areas.

In conclusion, while I agree that expenditure on railways is valuable, I believe that road infrastructure is ultimately more beneficial for most people in their daily lives, particularly for those living in rural and underserved areas.

利弊分析（Advantages/Disadvantages）— 网购

题型：Advantages/Disadvantages 核心框架：there are both positive and negative effects
Online shopping is becoming increasingly popular around the world. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?

It is true that an increasing number of people shop online as technology advances. This has affected individuals in various ways, and in my opinion there are both positive and negative effects.

Shopping online has had an impact on consumer behaviour and has broken through geographic boundaries in the relationship between consumers and retailers. Firstly, a consumer can buy products from all over the world on the internet without leaving home. Secondly, online prices are often lower than those in physical stores, making it more cost-effective for consumers. Thirdly, online shopping has also changed the way people compare products, as they can easily read reviews from other buyers before making a decision.

On the one hand, these developments can be extremely positive. In the past, it was much more difficult to find what you really wanted in local shops because the available choices were limited. Nowadays, shopping on computers or mobile phones is more convenient and offers a wider variety of choices. Many of us benefit from these services. For example, when individuals want to buy something specific online, it is often easier for them to compare prices online rather than walking around different stores to find the cheapest option.

On the other hand, the availability of discounts and promotions offered by online stores can also have the result of encouraging people to buy products that they do not actually need. For example, when people scroll through their phones, they often see discounts for their favourite clothing brands, which makes them buy impulsively. In addition, people who buy clothes or shoes online do not have the chance to try them on. This may lead to a higher risk of receiving inappropriate items.

In conclusion, online shopping has certainly revolutionised the way people consume products, but not all of the outcomes of this revolution have been positive.

原因 + 解决方案（Causes and Solutions）— 肥胖问题

题型：Causes & Solutions 核心框架：This essay will discuss… and the measures…
In many countries, the number of people suffering from obesity and weight-related health problems is increasing. What are the causes of this issue, and what can governments and individuals do to solve it?

Humans are responsible for a variety of weight-related health issues and obesity. They can also take steps to reduce weight. This essay will discuss weight-related health problems and the measures that governments and individuals can take to address these problems.

Two of the biggest causes of obesity and weight-related issues are the abundance of fast food and a sedentary lifestyle. Fast food can be easily found in communities, and many advertisements promote it, which has a devastating effect on individuals, especially children, who cannot resist the desire to try those tasty chips or fried chicken wings. As technology advances, many individuals also like to play video games or watch short videos on the internet, which can cause them to spend hours sitting in front of their screens, thereby leading to a sedentary lifestyle.

Governments could certainly make more efforts to tackle these issues. They could build more sports centres, which would make it easier for citizens to exercise, socialise, and stay active. They could also introduce laws to ban advertisements for fast food and impose taxes on fast food companies. In this way, companies might produce less fast food, and people would be less likely to be exposed to such advertisements, thereby benefiting from these regulations.

Individuals should also take responsibility for dealing with their health issues. They can do small amounts of exercise regularly, which is not too difficult to maintain. For example, individuals can take the stairs rather than using a lift, and choose to walk for short distances instead of driving or taking a taxi. Also, most people can reduce the time they spend sitting in front of screens. For instance, they could stand up and walk around for a few minutes every hour, with a reminder set on their devices. By doing regular exercise and avoiding long periods of sitting, they can help reduce the likelihood of obesity and weight-related issues in the long run.

In conclusion, both governments and individuals must pay more attention to health issues and take steps to address them. Only through joint efforts can we effectively reverse this worrying trend.

原因 + 解决方案（Causes and Solutions）— 厌学问题

题型：Causes & Solutions 核心框架：rooted in… / When learning is reduced to…
Many young people today leave school with negative attitudes towards learning. What are the reasons for this, and what can be done to encourage a positive attitude towards education?

Negative attitudes towards learning have become increasingly prevalent among school leavers. While the causes are complex, I believe they are largely rooted in excessive exam pressure and the perceived irrelevance of school subjects to real life.

The primary cause of such attitudes is the overwhelming exam pressure in many schools. Young students are required to take numerous tests and spend a disproportionate amount of time on rote memorisation. When learning is reduced to constant test preparation, students become passive recipients of information rather than active explorers, which often breeds a sense of school-weariness. Another significant factor is that many subjects are perceived as overly theoretical, offering little practical connection to students' daily lives. When students fail to see how classroom content applies to the real world, their motivation to learn inevitably diminishes.

To address these problems, schools have a crucial role to play. Firstly, schools should reduce the academic burden by eliminating unnecessary exams. It has been shown that when students are less burdened by frequent tests, they tend to develop greater intellectual curiosity. Without the constant pressure of exams, they are more willing to ask questions, explore topics in depth, and take intellectual risks—all of which are cornerstones of lifelong learning. Secondly, schools should introduce career-oriented courses that bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world applications. For example, a physics lesson on electricity could involve designing a simple circuit rather than merely memorizing formulas. When students clearly see how their learning translates into practical competence, their motivation shifts from merely passing exams to genuinely developing valuable skills.

In conclusion, negative attitudes towards learning are largely driven by exam-centric education and the perceived irrelevance of school subjects. By reducing excessive testing and connecting education to real-world skills, schools can transform learning from a burdensome chore into a meaningful pursuit. Ultimately, education should aim not merely to prepare students for exams, but to cultivate lifelong learners who carry a positive attitude beyond the classroom.

利大于弊（Outweigh）— 线上教育

题型：Outweigh 核心框架：more likely to have a positive impact
Online education has become increasingly popular in recent years. Do the advantages of online education outweigh the disadvantages?

As technology advances, many people enjoy taking online classes. While I accept that online education can sometimes have a negative effect, I believe that it is more likely to have a positive impact.

On the one hand, online education can lack interaction and require a high sense of self-discipline for learners. For example, when students attend an online class, they often find it difficult to stay focused, as there is no teacher physically present to monitor their attention. Unlike in a traditional classroom, where a raised hand can immediately get the teacher's response, online learners may have to wait hours or even days for a reply to their questions. This can lead to slow progress or even abandonment of the course, as students feel isolated and unsupported. Furthermore, without external supervision, learners must rely entirely on their own motivation to keep up with the coursework, which demands strong self-control that not all students possess.

However, I would argue that these drawbacks are outweighed by the advantages. Firstly, the lack of face-to-face interaction is less concerning than it might seem, as most online platforms now offer interactive tools such as WeChat groups, where students and teachers can exchange questions, ideas, and feedback in real time. In fact, such virtual spaces often encourage more participation than traditional classrooms, since quieter learners can contribute in writing rather than speaking publicly. Secondly, while online learning does demand self-discipline, it also helps students develop this valuable skill over time—a benefit that extends far beyond their academic lives. These advantages, particularly the enhancement of interaction and self-regulation, clearly outweigh the minor drawbacks.

In conclusion, it seems to me that the potential benefits of online education are more significant than the possible drawbacks.
