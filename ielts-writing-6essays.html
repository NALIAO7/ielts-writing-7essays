讨论双方（Discuss both views）— 远程办公

题型：Discuss both views 核心框架：While both arrangements have their merits, I believe…
Some people think that it is better to work from home, while others believe that working in an office is more beneficial. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

As technology advances, a growing proportion of the workforce now has the option to work remotely. While both arrangements have their merits, I believe that working from home offers greater overall benefits.

The option to work in an office is attractive for several reasons. Firstly, office-based work typically requires employees to adhere to a fixed schedule. This structure compels them to maintain a consistent sleep routine, which research has shown to benefit both physical and mental well-being. Moreover, the physical presence of colleagues and supervisors fosters a sense of accountability, which can effectively reduce procrastination and enhance productivity. For many, this structured and supervised environment provides a clear boundary between professional and personal life, helping them stay focused during working hours.

On the other hand, I believe that working from home offers greater overall benefits. Firstly, eliminating the daily commute allows employees to reclaim a significant amount of time. Instead of spending hours in traffic, they can invest this time in family, exercise, or personal pursuits—all of which contribute to a healthier work-life balance. Secondly, remote work offers greater flexibility, which directly improves job satisfaction. Employees can schedule demanding tasks during their most productive hours rather than conforming to a rigid nine-to-five routine. This autonomy not only boosts efficiency but also fosters a sense of trust and empowerment, often leading to higher motivation and lower stress levels.

In conclusion, while the office provides valuable structure and accountability, the flexibility and time savings offered by remote work make it the more advantageous option for many. As technology continues to dissolve the boundaries between work and home, the ability to work remotely is likely to become not just a perk, but a fundamental expectation of modern employment.

同意/不同意（Agree/Disagree）— 免费大学

题型：Agree/Disagree 核心框架：While I acknowledge the appeal… I believe its drawbacks outweigh…
Some people believe that university education should be free for all students, regardless of their financial background. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Some argue that university education should be free for all students, regardless of their financial background. While I acknowledge the appeal of this proposal, I believe its drawbacks would ultimately outweigh its benefits.

On the one hand, there are clear benefits to making university education free. Free tuition would remove the financial barrier that prevents talented but underprivileged students from pursuing higher education. By enabling them to acquire valuable qualifications and skills, this policy could help break the cycle of intergenerational poverty and promote social mobility. For these individuals, a university degree represents not just knowledge, but a genuine chance to transform their life prospects.

At the same time, I believe the drawbacks of free university education are far more significant. Firstly, making tuition free would place an enormous financial strain on the state. Since a government's budget is finite, channeling vast sums into higher education would inevitably mean diverting resources from other critical areas such as healthcare and infrastructure. Secondly, free education does not necessarily benefit the most disadvantaged. Wealthy families often have better access to private tutoring and elite secondary schools, which positions their children to capture the majority of free university places. In this way, a policy designed to level the playing field may ironically end up subsidising the education of the already privileged, while failing to reach those who truly need it.

In conclusion, while free university education may appear to be a just and progressive policy, the financial strain it places on public resources and its unintended consequence of favouring the privileged make it an impractical solution. Rather than making university free for all, governments should direct limited public funds toward targeted scholarships and improving primary and secondary education—measures that more effectively promote genuine equality of opportunity.

同意/不同意（Agree/Disagree）— 铁路 vs 公路

题型：Agree/Disagree 核心框架：While… are undeniably important, I disagree…
Some people believe that governments should spend more money on railways rather than on roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

While railways are undeniably important for intercity transport, I disagree with the view that governments should prioritise funding for railways over road infrastructure.

On the one hand, I acknowledge that railways offer clear advantages in specific scenarios. High-speed trains can transport large numbers of passengers over long distances with remarkable efficiency. For example, business travellers in China frequently rely on the high-speed network between Beijing and Shanghai, taking advantage of comfortable carriages equipped with tables and power outlets to work productively during the journey. However, such benefits are largely confined to intercity business travel and densely populated corridors. For the vast majority of ordinary citizens, particularly those in rural areas, railways are far less relevant to their daily transport needs.

At the same time, road construction is significantly more inclusive and cost-effective than railway expansion, especially in underdeveloped regions. The cost of building a kilometre of high-speed rail far exceeds that of constructing a road. For example, building two-lane roads is far more affordable and can immediately connect remote villages to towns, making it easier for children to attend school, farmers to transport their crops to markets, and patients to reach hospitals. Without such investment, these communities would remain isolated, widening the gap between urban and rural areas. In this way, road infrastructure delivers tangible and widespread benefits to ordinary citizens, particularly those in underserved rural communities.

In conclusion, while railways play a valuable role in connecting major cities, road infrastructure delivers far broader and more equitable benefits. Given limited public funds, governments should prioritise roads—a form of investment that reaches the greatest number of people and directly improves the daily lives of those who need it most.

利弊分析（Advantages/Disadvantages）— 网购

题型：Advantages/Disadvantages 核心框架：While this trend offers…, it also brings…
Online shopping is becoming increasingly popular around the world. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?

As technology advances, online shopping has become increasingly prevalent worldwide. While this trend offers considerable convenience, it also brings notable drawbacks.

On the positive side, online shopping offers several compelling advantages. Firstly, it eliminates geographical barriers, allowing consumers to purchase products from all over the world from the comfort of their own homes. Secondly, online prices are often significantly lower than those in physical stores, as e-retailers have lower overhead costs, making shopping far more cost-effective. Thirdly, the ability to read reviews from other buyers has transformed the way people compare products. Instead of relying solely on advertising or the advice of a single salesperson, consumers can now make more informed decisions based on the experiences of hundreds of fellow shoppers.

However, this trend is not without its downsides. The constant exposure to discounts, flash sales, and targeted advertisements often pushes consumers into impulse purchases. For instance, social media algorithms frequently tempt users into buying clothes or gadgets they had no intention of purchasing, leading to unnecessary spending and material waste. Furthermore, unlike in physical stores, online shoppers cannot try on items such as clothing or shoes before buying. This often results in receiving ill-fitting or unsuitable products, creating the added hassle of returns and exchanges. Over time, these frustrations can erode consumer trust and undermine the convenience that makes online shopping attractive in the first place.

In conclusion, while online shopping has brought unprecedented convenience and choice to consumers worldwide, it has also fuelled impulsive spending and created frustrations around product quality. The challenge for both retailers and consumers is to harness its benefits while mitigating its downsides.

原因 + 解决方案（Causes and Solutions）— 肥胖问题

题型：Causes & Solutions 核心框架：This troubling trend is largely driven by… Addressing it requires…
In many countries, the number of people suffering from obesity and weight-related health problems is increasing. What are the causes of this issue, and what can governments and individuals do to solve it?

The rising prevalence of obesity and weight-related health problems has become a pressing concern in many countries. This troubling trend is largely driven by the widespread availability of fast food and increasingly sedentary lifestyles. Addressing it requires coordinated action from both governments and individuals.

Two of the primary causes of rising obesity rates are the ubiquity of fast food and the prevalence of sedentary lifestyles. Fast food is now available on virtually every high street, and relentless advertising—often deliberately targeting children—makes it difficult for young people to resist. Lacking the experience to critically evaluate such marketing, children are particularly vulnerable to developing unhealthy eating habits early in life. At the same time, the rise of digital entertainment has encouraged increasingly inactive habits. Many individuals now spend hours sitting in front of screens, whether for gaming, streaming, or scrolling through social media. This not only displaces physical activity but also slows metabolism and encourages mindless snacking, all of which contribute directly to weight gain.

Governments have a range of policy tools at their disposal to address this crisis. Firstly, they could impose taxes on fast food companies and ban advertisements targeting children. Higher taxes would raise the price of unhealthy food, thereby reducing consumption, while advertising restrictions would limit children's exposure to persuasive marketing for nutritionally poor products. Over time, these measures could shift consumer preferences away from highly processed food. Secondly, governments could invest in public sports facilities, making it easier and more affordable for citizens to exercise regularly. When recreational spaces such as parks, running tracks, and community sports centres are widely accessible, physical activity becomes a natural part of daily life rather than a costly burden.

Individuals must also take proactive steps to safeguard their own health. Simple lifestyle adjustments—such as taking the stairs instead of the lift, or walking short distances rather than driving—can be seamlessly integrated into daily life without requiring significant time or expense. Additionally, people can reduce their screen time by setting regular reminders to stand up and move around every hour. These small but consistent actions, when sustained over time, can significantly lower the risk of obesity and related health conditions.

In conclusion, the rise in obesity is largely driven by the easy availability of fast food and sedentary habits. By implementing targeted policies and investing in public sports infrastructure, governments can create an environment that supports healthier choices. At the same time, individuals must adopt sustainable daily habits. Only through such coordinated efforts can this alarming trend be reversed.

原因 + 解决方案（Causes and Solutions）— 厌学问题

题型：Causes & Solutions 核心框架：rooted in… / When learning is reduced to…
Many young people today leave school with negative attitudes towards learning. What are the reasons for this, and what can be done to encourage a positive attitude towards education?

Negative attitudes towards learning have become increasingly prevalent among school leavers. While the causes are complex, I believe they are largely rooted in excessive exam pressure and the perceived irrelevance of school subjects to real life.

The primary cause of such attitudes is the overwhelming exam pressure in many schools. Young students are required to take numerous tests and spend a disproportionate amount of time on rote memorisation. When learning is reduced to constant test preparation, students become passive recipients of information rather than active explorers, which often breeds a sense of school-weariness. Another significant factor is that many subjects are perceived as overly theoretical, offering little practical connection to students' daily lives. When students fail to see how classroom content applies to the real world, their motivation to learn inevitably diminishes.

To address these problems, schools have a crucial role to play. Firstly, schools should reduce the academic burden by eliminating unnecessary exams. It has been shown that when students are less burdened by frequent tests, they tend to develop greater intellectual curiosity. Without the constant pressure of exams, they are more willing to ask questions, explore topics in depth, and take intellectual risks—all of which are cornerstones of lifelong learning. Secondly, schools should introduce career-oriented courses that bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world applications. For example, a physics lesson on electricity could involve designing a simple circuit rather than merely memorizing formulas. When students clearly see how their learning translates into practical competence, their motivation shifts from merely passing exams to genuinely developing valuable skills.

In conclusion, negative attitudes towards learning are largely driven by exam-centric education and the perceived irrelevance of school subjects. By reducing excessive testing and connecting education to real-world skills, schools can transform learning from a burdensome chore into a meaningful pursuit. Ultimately, education should aim not merely to prepare students for exams, but to cultivate lifelong learners who carry a positive attitude beyond the classroom.

利大于弊（Outweigh）— 线上教育

题型：Outweigh 核心框架：While this mode has certain drawbacks, I believe its advantages are more significant
Online education has become increasingly popular in recent years. Do the advantages of online education outweigh the disadvantages?

As technology advances, online education has gained widespread popularity. While this mode of learning has certain drawbacks, I believe its advantages are more significant.

On the one hand, online education can lack meaningful interaction and demands considerable self-discipline. Unlike in a traditional classroom, where a raised hand elicits an immediate response, online learners may wait hours or even days for answers to their questions. This delayed feedback can lead to frustration, slow progress, or even course abandonment, as students feel increasingly isolated and unsupported. Furthermore, without the physical presence of a teacher to maintain focus, learners must rely entirely on their own motivation—a challenge that requires a level of self-control not all students possess.

However, these drawbacks are outweighed by the advantages. Firstly, online education removes geographical barriers, granting students access to courses offered by prestigious institutions worldwide without the need to relocate. A learner in a rural area can now attend lectures by leading experts—something unimaginable in the pre-digital era. Secondly, online platforms offer greater flexibility than traditional classrooms. Students can learn at their own pace, pausing and replaying recorded lectures until they fully grasp the material. Interestingly, while online learning demands self-discipline, it also helps students develop this very skill—a benefit that extends far beyond academic life. Moreover, virtual platforms often encourage participation from quieter learners, who may feel more comfortable contributing in writing than speaking publicly.

In conclusion, while online education can suffer from delayed feedback and demands considerable self-motivation, its ability to transcend geographical boundaries and offer flexible, self-paced learning makes it a transformative force in modern education. As digital platforms continue to evolve, the remaining drawbacks are likely to diminish, making online learning an increasingly valuable option for students worldwide.
